# EVERYBODY HAS BEEF

Keep it. It should be the line for the whole franchise.

It works on three levels at the same time, and that's rare:

1. **Literal.** The Beef Tribe exists. It's marching on you.
2. **Street.** Everybody in this world has a grudge. Fruit against vegetable, grape against grape, landlord against tenant.
3. **Thematic.** That's what the show is about. Petty beef kept everybody small and divided, and the real beef showed up anyway.

A good tagline names the premise, the tone and the theme in three words. This one does all three and still sounds like something somebody would say out loud.

## How to use it

**Lock-up.** It always goes directly under the ANGRY FRUIT wordmark, in caps, with the period:

```
ANGRY FRUIT
EVERYBODY HAS BEEF.
```

**Teaser end card.** Run the four-word build first, then the line:

```
FRUIT. VEGETABLES. BEEF. PORK.
EVERYBODY HAS BEEF.
```

**Recurring device.** Every episode and every trailer ends with one small character undercutting the line in the dark. The teaser uses the grapes ("Technically, we don't." / "SHUT UP, GERALD."). Later tags can belong to other characters. It turns the line into a ritual the audience waits for.

**Faction variants** for posters, character one-sheets and social:

- Strawberry: *Some beef is personal. This beef is marching.*
- Avocado Divorce Attorney: *Everybody has beef. I bill hourly.*
- Pumpkin Landlord: *Everybody has beef. Rent is still due.*
- Lemon Relationship Therapist: *Everybody has beef. Let's talk about yours.*
- Pork Tribe, single poster, no character visible: *Everybody has beef. We have pork.*
- Beef Tribe, single poster, horns only: *Everybody has us.*

**Merch.** The line sells on its own, with no artwork needed. It also gives you a two-sided shirt: the line on the front, "SHUT UP, GERALD." on the back.

## Before spending money on it

Run a trademark clearance search on both **ANGRY FRUIT** and **EVERYBODY HAS BEEF** across entertainment (Class 41), apparel (Class 25) and toys/games (Class 28). File before the teaser goes public.
