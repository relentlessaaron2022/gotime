# ANGRY FRUIT

**EVERYBODY HAS BEEF.**

Angry Fruit is the name of the universe. It's not a cap on who gets to live there.

Fruit and vegetables have hated each other for generations. Property lines, market stalls, old insults, a feud over whether a tomato counts as either one. Then the Pork and Beef Tribes start marching, and Strawberry figures out what nobody in the orchard wanted to hear: produce has a common enemy, and it has been arguing with the wrong food group the whole time.

It's a premium limited series with the stakes played straight. Nobody in it knows they're funny.
